1. Introduction

Interext Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of the users ("User" or "You") of the Ander Baher™ mobile and web application ("App"). The App may be provided for use by your employer or another organisation authorised to use the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data. By downloading and using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate attendance, authentication, and leave management services, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information

When you are registered on the App by your employer or administrator, we collect:

  • Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth (DOB)
  • Gender

B. Biometric Data (Face Images)

We capture your facial image via the device camera or webcam during the registration process and when you mark your attendance (such as Punch In or Punch Out) for identity verification purposes. This section relates to attendance identity-verification images that we collect. It does not mean that we receive or store the raw fingerprint, face unlock template, device PIN, pattern, or screen-lock secret used locally by your device or passkey provider to unlock a Passkey.

  • Purpose: This data is used strictly for identity verification to prevent proxy attendance. On supported devices, face comparison may be performed locally on the device and, if the local result is inconclusive, through our fallback verification workflow.
  • Storage: Registered face profile images and attendance evidence may be securely transmitted and stored using Google Drive and related Google-hosted services used by the App. Live verification images are primarily processed for identity verification; if fallback verification is triggered, a captured image may also be transmitted securely through our backend workflow to Face++ for comparison.
  • Retention: Registered face profile data is generally retained while your account remains active, unless a longer retention period is required for official attendance records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

C. Location Data

We collect precise GPS location data (Latitude and Longitude) from your device.

  • When: We collect location data when you use attendance features such as Punch In, Punch Out, and regularisation. On supported devices and where enabled, we may also collect precise location in the background during an active attendance session after Punch In.
  • Purpose: We use this data to verify attendance at authorised locations, validate work-radius compliance during an active attendance session, and maintain attendance audit records.
  • Limits: Background location collection is intended to operate only during an active attendance session and is not used for advertising or unrelated profiling.

D. Attendance, Regularisation, and Related Records

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect attendance timestamps, punch type, work location references, reverse-geocoded addresses, attendance status, regularisation reasons, user remarks, manager remarks, and any supporting evidence or attachments that you choose to upload, such as images, PDFs, or audio files, where such features are enabled.

E. Passkey Credential and Sign-In Data

If You choose to enable or use passkey login on a supported device, browser, or credential provider, We may collect and maintain passkey-related authentication data such as credential identifiers, public key material, opaque user handles, organisation and account references, relying-party and app-surface identifiers, credential status or transport metadata, creation and last-used timestamps, correlation identifiers, and related security or audit records needed to register, verify, revoke, troubleshoot, or investigate passkey-based sign-in.

  • When: We collect this data when you enable, register, use, revoke, reset, or troubleshoot passkey login.
  • What We Do Not Receive: We do not receive or store the raw fingerprint, face unlock template, device PIN, pattern, or screen-lock secret used locally by your device, browser, password manager, security key, or other credential provider to unlock a Passkey.
  • Provider Behaviour: Depending on the credential provider you use, a Passkey may also be stored or synced by that provider across some of your devices in accordance with that provider's own terms, settings, and privacy practices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  1. Attendance Marking and Access Verification: To record your "In" and "Out" times accurately and to send, receive, and verify one-time passwords (OTPs) or similar access-verification details when you sign in or complete registration workflows.
  2. Geofence Validation: To ensure attendance is marked only from permitted locations.
  3. Identity Verification: To match your live photo with your registered profile photo using facial recognition technology, including local on-device comparison and, where required, fallback verification using our backend workflow and Face++.
  4. Regularisation: To alert you of missing punches and process regularisation requests, including the review of reasons, remarks, approvals, and any supporting evidence you choose to provide.
  5. Record Keeping: To generate attendance reports and exports for payroll and HR management.
  6. Passkey Authentication and Account Security: To offer optional passkey setup and sign-in, verify that a registered passkey belongs to your account, detect or reject invalid or revoked credentials, issue app sessions after successful verification, maintain security and audit records, and support fallback or recovery flows such as OTP verification.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage Locations: Depending on the feature used, your data may be processed and stored using Google-hosted services used by the App, including Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, and Google Drive. Where passkey login is enabled, passkey credential records and related security or audit records may also be processed through our secure authentication services and related backend infrastructure. Where fallback facial verification is triggered, relevant verification images may also be processed through Face++.
  • Security Measures: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including access controls and secure transmission practices, to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Certain limited data, such as sign-in state, organisation context, attendance-session settings, and passkey-setup or recovery state, may also be stored locally on your device to keep the App functioning properly.
  • Passkey Key Handling: For passkey login, the private key remains under the control of your device or credential provider. Our systems store only the server-side credential records, public-key-related material, and security metadata reasonably needed to register and verify passkey sign-in.
  • Disclaimer: While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to users or third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information with your organisation and its authorised administrators or managers where necessary to operate attendance, verification, reporting, regularisation, and account-security workflows. We may otherwise share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App, conducting our business, or serving our users, so long as those parties are engaged for legitimate business purposes and are expected to protect the information appropriately. This may include Google-hosted services used for storage and workflow operations, communication services used to send OTPs by email or SMS, authentication or security infrastructure used for passkey verification, and Face++ where fallback facial verification is required.
  • Credential Providers and Platform Services: If you choose to use passkey login, your device operating system, browser, password manager, security key provider, or other credential provider may separately process passkey creation, local user verification, storage, or sync functionality under that provider's own terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with state and central laws of India or respond to a court order.

6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have the right to request the deletion of your account.

  • How to Request: You can request account deletion by contacting care@anderbaher.com or through any account deletion request form that we or your organisation may make available.
  • What is Deleted: Upon a verified request, we will delete or de-identify your Profile Information (such as Name, Phone Number, Email, Gender, and DOB), your registered Biometric Data (such as stored face profile images), and registered passkey credential records associated with your account from our active systems, subject to reasonable backup cycles and any legal obligations.
  • What is Retained (Archived): You explicitly acknowledge and agree that official attendance and business records may be archived and retained, including Attendance Logs, punch-related Location records, attendance regularisation requests, user or manager remarks, supporting evidence or attachments linked to official records, passkey-related security or audit records, and other records reasonably required to administer attendance operations and account security.
  • Reason for Retention: These records may constitute official business, security, and employment records required for payroll processing, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, labour-law compliance, audit purposes, incident investigation, and related operational needs of your employer or organisation using the App.
