# Legal Release Notes - v1.3.0

- Effective Date: 2026-07-13

## Summary

- Updated Terms and Conditions to align with the passkey-enabled legal draft.
- Added a formal `Passkey` definition and expanded the service scope to cover secure sign-in and account verification.
- Added Terms language for optional passkey login, device credential security, recovery flows, and restrictions against bypassing authentication mechanisms.
- Updated Terms privacy, retention, liability, and disclaimer language to reflect passkey-related records and third-party credential-provider dependencies.
- Expanded the Privacy Policy to explain passkey credential and sign-in data, including what local biometric or device-secret data is not received or stored by the company.
- Added Privacy Policy disclosures for passkey-related usage, storage, sharing, deletion, retention, and credential-provider behavior alongside the existing OTP and fallback verification flows.
