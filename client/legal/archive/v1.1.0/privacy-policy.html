1. Introduction

Interext Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of the users ("User" or "You") of the Ander Baher Attendance mobile and web application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data. By downloading and using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate attendance and leave management services, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information

When you are registered on the App by your employer or administrator, we collect:

  • Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth (DOB)
  • Gender

B. Biometric Data (Face Images)

We capture your facial image via the device camera or webcam during the registration process and every time you mark your attendance (Punch In/Punch Out).

  • Purpose: This data is used strictly for identity verification to prevent proxy attendance.
  • Storage: Your facial images are securely transmitted and stored on our cloud storage servers hosted by Google Drive.
  • Retention: This data is retained as long as your account is active.

C. Location Data

We collect precise GPS location data (Latitude and Longitude) from your device.

  • When: We collect location data when you use attendance features such as Punch In, Punch Out, and regularisation. On supported devices and where enabled, we may also collect precise location in the background during an active attendance session after Punch In.
  • Purpose: We use this data to verify attendance at authorised locations, validate work-radius compliance during an active attendance session, and maintain attendance audit records.
  • Limits: Background location collection is intended to operate only during an active attendance session and is not used for advertising or unrelated profiling.

D. Device Information

We may collect information about the device you use to access the App, including the hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers, to ensure App compatibility and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  • Attendance Marking: To record your "In" and "Out" times accurately.
  • Geofence Validation: To ensure attendance is marked only from permitted locations.
  • Identity Verification: To match your live photo with your registered profile photo using facial recognition technology.
  • Regularization: To alert you of missing punches and process regularization requests.
  • Record Keeping: To generate attendance reports and exports for payroll and HR management.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage Locations: Your personal data and biometric images are stored on secure cloud servers provided by Google Drive and other industry-standard cloud providers.
  • Security Measures: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures (including encryption and access controls) to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  • Disclaimer: While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to users or third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App, conducting our business, or serving our users, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with state and central laws of India or respond to a court order.

6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have the right to request the deletion of your account.

  • How to Request: You can request account deletion via the App settings or by contacting care@anderbaher.com.
  • What is Deleted: Upon a verified request, we will permanently delete your Profile Information (Name, Phone Number, Email, Gender, DOB) and your Biometric Data (Face Images) from our active servers.
  • What is Retained (Archived): You explicitly acknowledge and agree that your Attendance Logs (Historical records of your Punch In/Out times and Locations) and Leave Records will be archived and retained.
  • Reason for Retention: These records constitute official business and employment records required for payroll processing, labor law compliance, and audit purposes by your employer (Interext Technologies Pvt. Ltd.).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at care@anderbaher.com
