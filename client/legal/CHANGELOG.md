This changelog tracks published legal document releases for AB Attendance.

## v1.3.0
- Effective Date: 2026-07-13
- Published Date: 2026-07-13
- Change Type: MINOR
- Documents Changed:
  - terms-and-conditions.html
  - privacy-policy.html
- Summary:
